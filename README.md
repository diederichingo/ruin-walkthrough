# Ruin renovation, Monsanto — 3D walkthrough

Static three.js walkthrough of Tom's stone ruin renovation (sloped ring beam design, 25 Nov 2026). Sketch, not for construction.
Same layout as the roundhouse walkthrough: Orbit / Walk, joystick on phones, sun by date and time, compass.
Extra toggles: Roof, Veranda, Steel + ring (hides the stone skins and clay band to show the ring beam and welded frame),
Cut (plan cut at 1.5 m, or a north–south section at x = 3.2 m), Heights (labels).

Local preview: `python3 -m http.server 8843 --bind 127.0.0.1` in this folder.
Geometry constants are at the top of `js/app.js` (same numbers as /workspace/ruin/draw11.py).
